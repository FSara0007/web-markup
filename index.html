Start Your Urban Greenhouse

Bring Nature Into Your Home

Living in a city can sometimes make you feel disconnected from nature, but it doesn't have to! even in you're a complete beginner, you don't need a garden or a lot of space to start growing plants. This website focuses on easy-to-grow plants that can thrive in apartments ans small living spaces

 

Urban Greenhouse is a simple guide for anyone who wants to bring more greenery into their home, especially people with little or no gardening experience. It provides beginner-friendly advice and helps you discover how easy it can be to grow plants in an urban enviorment. 

Start Simple

Choosing your first plant can feel overwhelming when you're not sure what to look for.

Some plants need lots of attention, while others are more forgiving if you forget to water them. Learning what a plant needs before buying one can help you find a plant that fits your home and your lifestyle.
