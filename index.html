Bring Nature Into Your Home

Living in a city can make you feel disconnected from nature, but it doesn't have to!  You don’t need a garden or a lot of space to start growing plants, this website focuses on plants that can thrive in any apartment.

 

This is a simple guide for any person, especially those who have no experience in gardening, who wants to bring more plants into their lives.

Start Simple

Choosing your first plant can feel overwhelming when you're not sure what to look for.

Some plants need lots of attention, while others are more forgiving if you forget to water them. Learning what a plant needs before buying one can help you find a plant that fits your home and your lifestyle.
