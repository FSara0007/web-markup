Welcome to Urban Greenhouse

This is a simple guide for anyone who wishes to bring more plants into their homes. You want to buy a houseplant but you don't know where to start? Or maybe you already have a few plants in your apartment, but you wish to learn more? Urban Greenhouse will help you with that.

Bring Nature Into Your Home

Living in cities can make you feel disconnected from nature, but it doesn't have to! You do not need a garden or a lot of space to start growing plants. This website focuses on plants that can thrive in any apartment.

Start Simple

Choosing your first plant can feel overwhelming when you're not sure what to look for.

Some plants need lots of attention, while others are more forgiving if you forget to water them. Learning what a plant needs before buying one can help you find a plant that fits your home and your lifestyle.
