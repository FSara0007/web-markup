Beginner Plants

If you wish to bring some greenery into your apartment but don't know where to start, there are plenty houseplants for people unexperienced with plant care. Here you can find a couple of beginner-friendly options that might suit your needs. 

1. Snake Plant

The snake plant is one of the most popular beginner indoor plants, and not for no reason. It’s one of the easiest plants to take care of, very forgiving when you forget about it.

  • Light: Low to bright indirect sunlight
  • Water: Only when the soil is completely dry

2. Ponytail Palm

Ponytail palms, or elephant foot palms, are long-lived indoor plants that are straightforward to grow, as long as you don’t overwater it. Ponytail palm prefers bright indirect light, ideally near a sunny window. However be warned to avoid harsh afternoon sunlight that can scorch leaves. Water deeply but infrequently. This plant is very capable of storing water so it’s easy to overwater it. Water it when the 5 cm of soil has dried.

  • Light: Bright indirect sunlight
  • Water: Water infrequently but deeply

3. ZZ Plant

ZZ plant, or also known as Zanzibar Gem and Eternity Plant, is also amazing choice for someone looking for low-maintenance plant.  The ZZ plant is well-suited for environments with low light and low humidity, which means you can place it whenever you want. It can also survive months without water.

  • Light: Any indirect light
  • Water: Water when soil is completely dry

4. Daisy Paintbrush

Daisy paintbrush is a succulent plant known for its low-maintenance requirements. The only real worry with this plant is having to be careful to not overwater it, because it requires minimal watering, you should allow the soil to dry out. This plant thrives in full sun, so if you can think of a place where it can get plenty of sunshine, this is a plant for you.

  • Light: Full light
  • Water: Every 3 weeks

A plant that fits you

Your home and lifestyle are important to take into consideration when it comes to choosing a houseplant.

Think about how much natural light is available in your apartment, where would you place your plants, and how much time you're willing to spend on plants.

There are no wrong answers, the right plant will be able to fit into your everyday life. 

Quick tip

Always check the soil before watering your plants!
